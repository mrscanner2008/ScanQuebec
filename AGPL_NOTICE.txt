This repository includes integration scaffolding intended to host a local
build of BrowSDR under live_sdr_vendor/.

BrowSDR and related upstream components may be under AGPL or other copyleft
licenses. Before distributing a bundled build:

1. Review the upstream BrowSDR repository license and notices.
2. Provide Corresponding Source for any AGPL-covered binaries you ship.
3. Keep attribution and license texts with the distributed package.

Remote iframe usage of https://jlx.brownsdr.com does not by itself create a
bundled derivative; local builds in live_sdr_vendor/ do.
